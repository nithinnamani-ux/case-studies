# Lab Reports — Case Studies

The six case studies from my UX Lab, written up the way they actually happened: the first guess, what the research turned up, the versions that failed testing, and what made the final cut. Every report runs the same evidence-to-outcome arc — interviews, surveys, affinity mapping, competitive analysis, three fidelity rounds, usability testing, final system, results — framed by the Double Diamond.

All six read in full, free, in the Work section of [nithinnamani.com](https://nithinnamani.com).
